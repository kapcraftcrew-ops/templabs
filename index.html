What the worker's phone knows

This tab uses real current weather for a place you choose and your own inputs. There is no simulated worker here. It is an estimate for the next block of work, not a measurement of your body and not medical advice.

Supervisors see conditions and crew totals. They never see a named worker's temperature, heart rate or alert history. Groups smaller than 5 are hidden and counts are rounded to the nearest 10%.

Plan the next work block

Tests candidate work and rest cycles against every crew member's twin with randomised uncertainty. It picks the schedule that keeps the most work in while holding the chance of passing 38.5 °C to 5% or less.

Press the button to plan from the selected time.

Emergency escalation

An individual is identified only after a severe symptom report or an emergency-level alert. The release goes to the site medic, lasts 60 minutes and is logged.
